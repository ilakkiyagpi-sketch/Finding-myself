# Future Self & Dreams ("Compass")

## Core idea for the app
People who feel connected to their **future self** make better long-term
choices. Dreams for mothers must be **season-aware**: a dream can be paced,
shrunk to a seed, or run alongside caregiving — not abandoned or forced.

## Source books & research

| Book | Author (year) | Key idea to use |
|---|---|---|
| *Future Self* | Benjamin Hardy (2022) | Clarify your future self; let it guide present decisions; the future self shapes the present more than the past does. |
| Future-self continuity research | Hal Hershfield (*Your Future Self*, 2023) | When people feel the future self is "a stranger", they neglect it; vivid connection (letters, images) increases long-term action. |
| *Rethinking Positive Thinking* | Gabriele Oettingen (2014) | **WOOP**: Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan. Mental contrasting beats pure positive fantasy. |
| *Designing Your Life* | Burnett & Evans (2016) | **Odyssey Plans**: three different 5-year lives (current path, if that disappeared, if money/image didn't matter); prototype small. |
| *Working Identity* | Herminia Ibarra (2003) | Try out new selves through small experiments, new networks, and sense-making. |
| *Big Magic* | Elizabeth Gilbert (2015) | Creative living beyond fear; you don't need permission or perfect conditions to make things. |
| *Find Your Unicorn Space* | Eve Rodsky (2021) | Protect time for a self-expressive pursuit; tie it to a goal and share it with others. |
| *Atomic Habits* | James Clear (2018) | Goals are reached through systems; identity statements bridge present to future self. |
| *Four Thousand Weeks* | Oliver Burkeman (2021) | Choose a few things that matter; patience and finitude over hustle. |

## Compass module mechanics
1. **Dream inventory:** dreams from past chapters (revived) + new dreams. Each tagged: *revive / evolve / new*.
2. **Seed → Sprout → Grow** sizing: a dream can be held at seed level (15 min/week) during heavy seasons.
3. **WOOP card** per dream.
4. **Odyssey plans** (3 versions) for the next 5 years; rate each for energy, confidence, coherence with values.
5. **Letter from future me** (1, 5, 10 years) — AI helps her write it in her own voice; re-read at milestones.
6. **Monthly tiny experiment** toward a dream (Ibarra).
7. **Roadmap** that respects roles: milestones automatically checked against the Roles Balancer load.
8. **"In my own way" rule:** the AI never imposes a standard success path; it asks what success means *to her*.

## Prompt bank
- What dream did you put on the shelf? Is it still yours, or has it changed shape?
- Imagine yourself at 50, living fully as yourself. Describe an ordinary Tuesday.
- What's the smallest version of this dream you could start this month?
- What obstacle inside you is most likely to stop this? What will you do when it shows up?
