# Values, Strengths & Purpose

## Core idea for the app
Under all the roles, a person has a fairly stable core: **values** (what
matters), **strengths** (what energises and comes naturally) and **sources of
meaning**. Roles change; the core can be re-expressed. "Finding myself" is
largely re-connecting daily life to that core.

## Source books

| Book | Author (year) | Key idea to use |
|---|---|---|
| *The Happiness Trap* | Russ Harris (2007) | ACT (Acceptance & Commitment Therapy): values are chosen life directions (a compass, not a destination). Take small **committed actions** toward values even while hard feelings are present. |
| *Authentic Happiness* | Martin Seligman (2002) | Using **signature strengths** daily leads to deeper satisfaction than pleasure alone. |
| *Flourish* | Martin Seligman (2011) | **PERMA** model of wellbeing: Positive emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Accomplishment. |
| *Character Strengths and Virtues* | Christopher Peterson & Martin Seligman (2004) | The **VIA** classification: 24 strengths under 6 virtues (wisdom, courage, humanity, justice, temperance, transcendence). Free VIA survey exists. |
| *Flow* | Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi (1990) | Optimal experience happens when challenge matches skill and attention is absorbed. Flow activities are clues to the true self. |
| *Ikigai* | Héctor García & Francesc Miralles (2016) | Purpose at the overlap of what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, what sustains you; small daily purpose matters. |
| *Designing Your Life* | Bill Burnett & Dave Evans (2016) | Design thinking for life: **Good Time Journal** (log energy & engagement per activity), **Workview/Lifeview**, **Odyssey Plans** (3 alternative 5-year lives), prototype conversations. |
| *Finding Your Own North Star* | Martha Beck (2001) | Distinguish the **essential self** (instinct, desire, joy) from the **social self** (skills to meet expectations). Body signals ("shackles on / shackles off") point toward the essential self. |
| *The Way of Integrity* | Martha Beck (2021) | Suffering grows when life is out of alignment with what you truly believe; re-align step by step. |
| *Mindset* | Carol Dweck (2006) | Growth mindset: abilities develop; useful for "I've lost my skills" fears. |

## Self-dimensions (used by the app's Self Graph)
Score each 0–10, captured as **Past (chosen chapter)**, **Present**, and **Desired**:
1. Creativity & play
2. Learning & curiosity
3. Body & health
4. Solitude & inner life
5. Friendships & belonging
6. Partnership / intimacy
7. Work, contribution & ambition
8. Spirituality / meaning
9. Independence & agency
10. Joy & fun
11. Rest
12. Appearance & self-expression (how I present myself)

Gap = Desired − Present. "Missing piece" = high in Past or Desired, low in Present.

## App mechanics derived
- **Values card sort:** ~40 value cards → sort into Very / Somewhat / Not important → pick top 5. Re-run every 3–6 months to show change.
- **Strengths:** offer link to free VIA survey, or a short in-app strengths reflection; store top 5.
- **Good Time Journal:** tag each logged activity with energy (+/−) and engagement — feeds the routine analyser.
- **Essential vs social self:** for activities, ask "Do I do this because I want to, or because I should?"
- **Values–time alignment score:** compare top values to how time is actually spent.
- **Committed action of the week:** one small action per top value.

## Prompt bank
- Which activities make you lose track of time? Which did as a child?
- If no one would judge you, how would you spend a free Sunday?
- Which value of yours has been quietest since you became a mother? How could it get 10 minutes this week?
- What do people always come to you for?
