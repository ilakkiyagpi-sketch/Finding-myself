# Roles, Balance, Boundaries & Burnout

## Core idea for the app
Balance is not equal time per role; it's **conscious allocation** across a
season, with the **Self** counted as a real role. Burnout comes from
uncompleted stress cycles, invisible labour and a lack of boundaries — not
personal weakness.

## Source books

| Book | Author (year) | Key idea to use |
|---|---|---|
| *Burnout: The Secret to Unlocking the Stress Cycle* | Emily & Amelia Nagoski (2019) | Deal with the **stress** (body) separately from the **stressor**. Complete the stress cycle: physical activity, breathing, positive social contact, laughter, affection, crying, creative expression. "Human giver syndrome": women taught to give everything. |
| *Fair Play* | Eve Rodsky (2019) | Make domestic labour visible; whoever holds a task owns conception, planning and execution; renegotiate with partner/family. |
| *Overwhelmed* | Brigid Schulte (2014) | "Time confetti" — leisure fragmented by mental load; cultural and structural causes of mothers' time scarcity. |
| *Drop the Ball* | Tiffany Dufu (2017) | Let go of unrealistic expectations of doing it all; delegate and accept "good enough". |
| *Unfinished Business* | Anne-Marie Slaughter (2015) | Value care as much as career; the problem is structural, not individual. |
| *Set Boundaries, Find Peace* | Nedra Glover Tawwab (2021) | Boundaries are expectations that keep you safe and well; types (physical, emotional, time, material…); say them clearly, without over-explaining. |
| *The Mental Load* (comic) | Emma (2018) | Illustrates being the household "project manager". |
| *Sacred Rest* | Saundra Dalton-Smith (2017) | Seven rest types — burnout is often a mismatch of rest type. |
| Role theory (sociology) | Goode (role strain, 1960) | Role strain/conflict when demands of multiple roles exceed resources. |

## Roles Balancer — app mechanics
1. **Role inventory:** she lists her roles (Mother, Wife, Daughter, Daughter-in-law, Employee/Entrepreneur, Friend, Community member, **Me**).
2. For each role: hours/week, energy given vs. received, satisfaction (0–10), "who I want to be in this role" (one line).
3. **Role wheel chart** — current vs desired.
4. **Load map (Fair Play-style):** tasks with C/P/E ownership; highlight what can be shared, simplified, dropped or delegated.
5. **Boundary builder:** pick a situation → AI drafts a kind, clear boundary script (in her language/tone; can be Tamil/English etc.).
6. **Burnout check (monthly):** exhaustion, cynicism/detachment, reduced sense of efficacy (self-report scale inspired by burnout research — not diagnostic).
7. **Stress-cycle completer:** quick menu (20 min walk, 6 deep breaths, hug for 20 seconds, a good cry, dance to one song).
8. **"Me" role is never zero:** the weekly planner warns if Self time is empty.

## Prompt bank
- Which role takes the most from you right now? Which gives you the most back?
- Which task do you carry in your head that no one else sees?
- What is one thing you can drop, delegate or do "good enough" this week?
- What would a boundary sound like that protects your energy and still honours your love?
