# Finding Myself

A self-reflection companion that helps women — especially mothers juggling many roles —
**rewind** who they were, **see** who they are now, **find the missing pieces**,
**balance** their roles, and **move toward their own dreams in their own way**.

- 🦴 App skeleton: [`docs/APP_BLUEPRINT.md`](docs/APP_BLUEPRINT.md)
- 📚 Books behind it: [`docs/READING_LIST.md`](docs/READING_LIST.md)
- 🧠 Knowledge skills: [`.claude/skills/`](.claude/skills/) — one per theme (motherhood, self-awareness, life story, values, habits, roles & burnout, emotions, future self, safety)
